# KIPP-CAI Project Charter (draft for class ratification)

**Mission:** Build a reusable, verified commons of AI prompts, skills, workflows and academic problem-solving experiences. Communication and the community are primary; the app is supporting infrastructure.

**Participants:** ~60+ KIPP students, 10 functional teams, three weeks before final exams.

**TAIDA:** Target (collaborative capacity), Attention (lost learning), Interest (practical benefit), Desire (reusable resources), Action (three-week commitments).

**Target outputs:** 25 prompt drafts, 15 skill proposals; **acceptance minimum:** 10 tested prompts, 6 tested skills (one per category), one functional catalogue, one integrated showcase, peer review and handover.

**Shared values:** respectful communication; equitable participation; verification; attribution; privacy; accessible tooling; constructive criticism.

**Decision rights:** instructor approves pedagogical/assessment changes; steering council prioritizes work; domain reviewers curate claims; code maintainers approve merges; students own personal consent.

**Non-goals:** paid AI subscriptions, production-grade SaaS, widespread collection of Telegram chat history, compulsory AI use, academic dishonesty.

**Approval:** instructor and ten team coordinators to confirm names, dates and permissions. This file remains a draft until signed/ratified.
